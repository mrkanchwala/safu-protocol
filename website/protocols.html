Run this pool
under your own name.

SAFU gives protocols, wallet providers, and platforms holding user funds a way to protect their users, individuals or autonomous agents alike, from phishing, approval exploits, and key compromise. An ecosystem can deploy the same pool one level up, protecting the protocols built on it instead: when one is hacked, the payout goes to the protocol itself, fast enough to stop a bank run before it starts. Neither flow has a vote or a claim form, because the contract enforces the payout rule itself.

For protocols, wallet providers & platforms

Protect your own users' wallets.

You hold your users' funds, or your users hold their own wallet through your product. A verified drain triggers an automatic payout, up to a set multiple of the stake, sized to the wallet's risk tier and never more than the loss itself.

Your pool, your brand.

Your users stake through your product and the pool carries your name. The engine underneath is ours, so you ship coverage without building a claims system to run it.

Wallet-level loss covered.

Phishing, approval exploits and key compromise are how wallets actually get drained, and the pool covers them whether a person or an autonomous agent runs the wallet.

No vote, no claim form.

The contract enforces the payout rule itself, and the same loss event always produces the same calculation. Nobody sits in a room deciding who deserves it.

Nothing collected upfront.

Your users pay nothing upfront. A staker who claims forfeits their principal, and that forfeiture is what funds the payout.

For ecosystems & chains

Protect the protocols in your ecosystem.

A chain or a foundation can also deploy one level up, with a pool that covers the protocols built on its ecosystem. It works like a reserve ratio: when a protocol in the pool is hacked, the payout goes to the protocol itself, deterministically and fast, so it can absorb the loss and keep honoring withdrawals. Their end-users are protected because the protocol stays solvent.

Stops a bank run before it starts.

The payout lands fast enough that the protocol keeps honoring withdrawals, so its users never see a locked balance.

Protocol-level exploits covered.

Oracle and governance manipulation: the attacks an audit looks for, covered for the day one slips through.

Member protocols stake in too.

Protocols in the pool stake into a shared reserve, with optional seed capital from the launching ecosystem. It runs on the same forfeiture-funded mechanism as the wallet-level pools, one level up.

Isolated per deployment.

Each pool is its own contract instance, capitalized independently. A new partner's pool never exposes another partner's stakers.

Deploy on your chain

Built once, ported per chain.

The engine already runs on two very different execution models, Ethereum's EVM and Stellar's Soroban, with the same deterministic mechanism underneath both. Those two are the proof that it ports, and more chains can follow.

One engine for every chain.

The same deterministic scoring and payout mechanism runs on Ethereum's EVM and Stellar's Soroban today. A new chain is a new deployment of the same engine.

A security review per chain.

Every new deployment goes through its own security review before it takes real stakes.
